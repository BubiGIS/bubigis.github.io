About

I hold a BSc Honours degree in Geography and Population Studies (2.1) from Lupane State University and have worked in local government, electoral mapping and research. My days are split between desktop GIS, field data collection and flying drones for aerial survey.

I lead teams, write the reports, and care about costs as much as accuracy. Good environmental stewardship and good budgets usually point the same way.
